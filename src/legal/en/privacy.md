**In short:** this website does not collect, store or share your personal data. If you email me, I use your message only to reply.

## Who is responsible

Darko Boesmans, private individual, Belgium. Contact: [darkoboesmans@gmail.com](mailto:darkoboesmans@gmail.com).

## What this website collects

Nothing. There are no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no ads and no embedded content from other companies. Fonts and icons are served from this website itself, so loading a page does not contact Google or any other third party.

## Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). Like any web server, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you load a page, to keep the service secure and running. I have no access to those logs. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## The contact form

The form does not send anything to a server. The button opens your own mail app with your message filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send in your mail app.

When you email me, I receive your email address, your name if your mail app includes it, and your message. I use these only to reply to you.

- **Legal basis:** my legitimate interest in answering messages sent to me (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), or steps you ask me to take before a possible collaboration (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **Where it is stored:** my mailbox at Gmail (Google). See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **How long:** I delete conversations that do not lead to a collaboration within 12 months of the last message. If we work together, I keep them as long as needed for that work and for legal obligations.
- I never sell your data, use it for marketing or add you to a mailing list. There is no newsletter.

## Settings stored on your device

The site remembers your language and theme choice in your browser. See the [cookie policy](/en/cookies/).

## Children

This site is not aimed at children. If you are under 13, ask a parent or guardian before emailing me.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive a copy. Email me with the subject "Privacy request". I reply within one month.

**Delete my data:** email the subject "Delete my data" from the address you used. I delete our conversation and confirm within one month.

If you think I handle your data incorrectly, you can complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority, Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels, [dataprotectionauthority.be](https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be).

## Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
