This website does not use cookies.

## What is stored instead

When you pick a language or theme yourself, the site saves that choice in your browser's local storage:

| Name | Value | Why | How long |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `db-lang` | `en` or `nl` | Remembers the language you picked | Until you clear your browser data |
| `db-theme` | `light` or `dark` | Remembers the theme you picked | Until you clear your browser data |

These values stay on your device. They are never sent to me or anyone else.

## Why there is no cookie banner

EU and Belgian rules require consent before information is stored on your device, unless it is strictly necessary for a service you explicitly asked for. Remembering a language or theme you chose yourself falls under that exception. There is no tracking to consent to.

## Removing the settings

Clear this site's data in your browser settings. The site then follows your system theme and browser language again.

## Third parties

This site loads nothing from other companies: no analytics, ads, social media widgets, video embeds or external fonts.
